Privacy Policy

Voice Recorder

Voice Recorder is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our voice recording application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Audio Recordings

  • When you use our app to record audio, the recordings are created and stored locally on your device.
  • We do not upload, transmit, or share your audio recordings to any external servers.

1.2 Permissions We Request

Our app may request the following permissions:

Permission Purpose
RECORD_AUDIO Required to capture audio using your device's microphone
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required to show recording status and controls in notifications
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Required to continue recording when the app is in the background
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION Required to record device audio during screen capture

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Local storage only: App settings and recording metadata (file names, durations) are stored locally on your device.
  • Crash logs: If the app crashes, anonymous crash reports may be collected to help us improve the app's stability. These reports do not contain personal information or audio content.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Primary Use

  • Your audio recordings are used solely for the purpose you intend—to record and play back sounds on your device.
  • Recordings are stored in your device's local storage and remain under your control.

2.2 No Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your audio recordings with any third parties.
  • We do not use your recordings for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

  • All audio files and app data are stored locally on your device.
  • You can delete recordings at any time through the app or your device's file manager.

3.2 Security Measures

  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data on your device.
  • However, please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Your Rights and Choices

4.1 Access and Control

  • You have full control over your recordings.
  • You can play, rename, share, or delete recordings at any time.

4.2 Uninstall

  • Uninstalling the app will remove all app data, including recordings stored in the app's directory.
  • Note: Recordings saved in public folders may need to be deleted manually.

4.3 Permissions

  • You can revoke any permissions granted to the app through your device's Settings > Apps > Sound Recorder > Permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version posted on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
